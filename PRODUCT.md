# KinderNewGenz

## Product Truth
KinderNewGenz is a multi-tenant SaaS kindergarten management platform.
Engineered for school administrators, teachers, and parents.
Backbone: Bun + Hono + Drizzle ORM + PostgreSQL with Row-Level Security (RLS).
Frontend: High-craft dashboard and landing surface.

## Target Audience
- **School Administrators:** Daily operations, enrollment, student migrations, billing, staff oversight.
- **Teachers:** Attendance, class timeline, daily journals, child activity logs.
- **Parents:** Real-time updates, child timeline, attendance notifications, fee payment.
- **System Admin:** Tenant provisioning, support sessions, audit logs.

## Visual Identity & Design Direction
- **Mode by Surface:**
  - Landing Page: `Persuade` (high trust, premium modern education, decisive action).
  - App Dashboard: `Operate` (dense scanability, high utility, collapsible navigation, zero cognitive drag).
- **Aesthetic World:** Executive Trust & Modern Playful Precision. No childish kitsch; no generic purple AI gradients; no card-in-card nesting.
- **Color Tokens:**
  - Primary Navy: `#023664` (deep trust, structured chrome, primary headings).
  - Warm Gold Accent: `#FAD105` (CTA highlights, active indicators, achievement badges).
  - Neutral Base / Canvas: `#F8FAFC` to `#FFFFFF` (tinted cool slate, no dead #000000 or raw gray).
  - Surface Neutral: `#E2E8F0` / `#E6E6E6` for borders and dividers.
- **Typography:**
  - Primary Display & Headings: `Plus Jakarta Sans` or `Outfit` (warm, geometric, highly legible).
  - UI Body & Numbers: `Plus Jakarta Sans` with tabular figures (`tnum`) for operational data.
  - Ban: Generic `Inter`, `Roboto`, `Arial`.
- **Motion Principles:**
  - Operational speed: 150ms–220ms ease-out for dropdowns, drawers, collapsible sidebar transitions.
  - Landing interactions: Smooth staggered reveals (Framer Motion), subtle floating perspective mockups, no bouncing/spring rubber-banding.
